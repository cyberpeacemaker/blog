**Yes, absolutely!** You can easily save a clean duplicate of your presentation without the highlighter/ink mark.

Here is the best way to do it:

### Step 1: Save a New Copy First

Before stripping the marks, make sure to save a new file so you don't overwrite your annotated version:

1. Go to **File** $\rightarrow$ **Save a Copy** (or **Save As**).
    
2. Give it a new name (e.g., `Presentation_Clean.pptx`).
    

### Step 2: Remove the Highlighter Mark

Choose whichever method is easiest for you:

#### Method A: The Quickest Way (Strips ALL Ink in the File)

1. Go to **File** $\rightarrow$ **Info**.
    
2. Click **Check for Issues** $\rightarrow$ **Inspect Document**.
    
3. Make sure **Ink** is checked in the list, then click **Inspect**.
    
4. Click **Remove All** next to _Ink_.
    

#### Method B: Using the Ribbon Menu

1. Click the **Review** tab at the top.
    
2. Look for the **Delete** option under the Ink section.
    
3. Choose **Delete All Ink on Slide** (or **Delete All Ink in Presentation**).
    

#### Method C: Manual Removal

1. Click directly on the yellow highlighter stroke on the slide.
    
2. Press the **Delete** key on your keyboard.
    

Once you've removed the mark, hit **Ctrl + S** (or **Cmd + S** on Mac) to save your clean copy!
