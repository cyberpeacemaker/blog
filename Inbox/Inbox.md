# Inbox

Temporary staging for unsorted captures. Topic notes land here via **New note** (`Ctrl+N`); triage them to the right topic folder during weekly review.

- **Daily journal** lives in `daily/` (Calendar or `Ctrl+Shift+D`) — not in this folder
- **`created`** in Properties is the date source of truth — no date prefix in topic filenames
