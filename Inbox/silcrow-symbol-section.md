That handy little symbol is called the **section sign** (or "silcrow"): **§**.

Here is how to type it depending on your device or program:

## 💻 On Computers

- **Mac:** Press **`Option` + `6`**
    
- **Windows (Numeric Keypad):** Hold **`Alt`** and type **`0167`** (or **`21`**) on your numpad, then release `Alt`.
    
- **Windows (No Numpad):** Press **`Win` + `.`** (period) to open the symbol picker, click the **Ω (Symbols)** tab, and search for it.
    

## 📱 On Mobile Devices

- **iPhone / iPad (iOS):**
    
    1. Tap the **`123`** key.
        
    2. Tap the **`#+=`** key.
        
    3. Tap **`§`** (it's right on the keyboard layout).
        
        _(Alternative: Long-press the **`&`** key)._
        
- **Android (Gboard):**
    
    1. Tap the **`?123`** key.
        
    2. Tap the **`=\<`** key.
        
    3. Tap **`§`**.
        
        _(Alternative: Long-press the paragraph symbol **`¶`** or the letter **`s`**)._
        

## 📄 For Coding & Word Processing

- **Microsoft Word:** Press **`Ctrl` + `Alt` + `S`** (or type `00A7` and press `Alt` + `X`).
    
- **HTML:** Use `&sect;` or `&#167;`
    
- **LaTeX:** Use `\S`
