You can find it quickly by using Gmail's search operators.

Paste either of these search queries into the Gmail search bar at the top (replace `receiver@example.com` with the person's email address or name):

- **Best standard search:**
    
    `to:receiver@example.com subject:fwd`
    
- **If you might have changed the subject line:**
    
    `to:receiver@example.com "Forwarded message"`
    

### Useful Search Tweaks

- **Search only your Sent folder:** Since you forwarded it, the message will definitely be in your Sent folder.
    
    `in:sent to:receiver@example.com subject:fwd`
    
- **If you remember a specific keyword from the original email:**
    
    `to:receiver@example.com subject:fwd "keyword"`
