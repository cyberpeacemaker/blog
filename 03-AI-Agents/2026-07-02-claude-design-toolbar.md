This toolbar snippet is from **Claude Design**, Anthropic’s workspace for building visual prototypes, landing pages, and slide decks.

These three options are your core tools for reviewing, adjusting, and collaborating on the visual canvas that Claude generates.

---

### 1. Mark up

* **What it does:** This activates a point-and-click (or sketch) annotation mode.
* **How it works:** Instead of typing a long instruction in a chat box, you can click directly on a specific element on the canvas (like a button, heading, or image) and write a direct instruction for it (e.g., *"Make this font bolder"* or *"Move this button to the right"*). Claude will read your markup and surgically update just that section.

### 2. Comments

* **What it does:** This opens the collaborative review and feedback panel.
* **How it works:** Much like comments in Figma or Google Docs, this is where you or your team members can leave general notes, feedback, or task threads on the design. It is ideal for team reviews where multiple stakeholders need to sign off or request changes without immediately triggering an AI redesign.

### 3. Show tweaks

* **What it does:** This toggles the visibility of quick-adjustment controls, global variables, or design changes.
* **How it works:** Claude Design generates automated visual sliders and quick toggles for things like accent colors, grid spacing, font sizing, and layout densities. Clicking **Show tweaks** reveals or hides these quick controls so you can rapidly experiment with different global variations of your design.
