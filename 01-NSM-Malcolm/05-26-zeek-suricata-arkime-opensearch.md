Here is a breakdown of exactly what is happening in the diagram to confirm and expand on your thoughts:

### **1. Zeek & Suricata (The Network Sensors)**

* **What they do:** As you mentioned, they both take the raw **PCAP** (Packet Capture) as input. They act as the "eyes" on the network.
* **Zeek:** Acts like a network metadata engine. It doesn't just look for bad things; it logs *everything* (connections, DNS requests, HTTP headers). Notice the **Carved File Scan** above it? Zeek is also extracting files from the network traffic and sending them to tools like ClamAV, YARA, CAPA, and VirusTotal to check for malware.
* **Suricata:** Acts primarily as an Intrusion Detection System (IDS). It uses rules and signatures to spot known malicious behavior in the PCAP and generates alerts.
* **The Pipeline:** The useful information (logs and alerts) generated by Zeek and Suricata is collected by **Filebeat**, forwarded to **Logstash** for processing, and ultimately deposited into **OpenSearch**.

### **2. Arkime (Capture & Analysis)**

* **What it does:** You are entirely correct. Arkime (formerly Moloch) is designed to make large-scale PCAP data searchable and analyzable.
* **Arkime Capture:** This component reads the PCAP (or listens to live traffic interfaces to *create* the PCAP). It stores the raw packet data on disk and parses the traffic to extract rich session metadata (SPI data). It sends this metadata directly to OpenSearch.
* **Arkime Viewer:** This is the web interface. When an **Analyst** wants to dig deep into a specific network session, they use the Arkime Viewer. **Because the metadata is indexed in OpenSearch, the analyst can run lightning-fast searches and then pivot to view the actual raw packets.**

### **3. The Central Hub (OpenSearch)**

* **The Red Circle:** The area circled in red highlights the core data storage and visualization backend. **OpenSearch** acts as the central brain, storing the Zeek logs, the Suricata alerts, and the Arkime session data all in one place.
* **OpenSearch Dashboards:** This gives the analyst a high-level, graphical view (pie charts, graphs) of everything happening across all the tools.

In short: Zeek and Suricata tell you *what* happened, OpenSearch stores and organizes it, and Arkime allows you to drill down into the exact *packets* to see the hard evidence.
