If you’ve ever seen or used Discord, you already know 80% of how Slack looks and feels. They both use the exact same layout: a sidebar of channels on the left and a big chat window in the middle.

However, while they look like twins, they have completely different **personalities and purposes**.

Here is the easiest way to think about the difference:

> 🏢 **Slack is a Corporate Office Building.** It is built for professional teams, productivity, tracking tasks, and getting work done.
> 🎮 **Discord is a Community Clubhouse or Living Room.** It was originally built for video gamers and is designed for hanging out, casual chatting, and voice streaming.

---

## Slack vs. Discord: At a Glance

| Feature | Slack (Office) | Discord (Clubhouse) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Main Goal** | Work, project management, and business communication. | Gaming, hobbies, and hanging out with friends. |
| **The "Vibe"** | Clean, professional, and quiet. | Casual, fun, and energetic. |
| **Voice/Video** | You usually have to "start a call" or a meeting, just like Zoom. | They have "Voice Channels" where you can just sit inside a room, and friends can pop in and out to chat anytime. |
| **AI & Tools** | Connects to tools like **Claude**, Google Drive, and spreadsheets. | Connects to music bots, gaming stats, and community moderation tools. |

---

## Why this matters for you

Because they are so similar, your comfort level with Discord will transfer right over to Slack. The biggest shift is just mental: on Discord, people might use gaming nicknames and post memes all day; on Slack, people use their real names and focus on getting a project finished so everyone can log off for the weekend.
