The relationship between these entities is that **VirusTotal** and **Mandiant** are the foundational data and intelligence engines that power the unified **Google Threat Intelligence** platform.

Here is a straightforward breakdown of how they all fit together to create a single cybersecurity offering:

### The Pillars of Google Threat Intelligence

In May 2024, Google merged three distinct streams of cybersecurity data, tools, and expertise to launch Google Threat Intelligence.

* **Mandiant (Frontline Expertise):** Acquired by Google in 2022, Mandiant is renowned for its elite incident response teams that investigate the world's most severe cyber breaches. They provide the platform with deep, human-driven insights into how advanced threat actors, hacker groups, and nation-states operate on the ground.
* **VirusTotal (Crowdsourced Intelligence):** Google acquired VirusTotal in 2012. It serves as the world's largest crowdsourced malware and threat repository. Because millions of files, domains, and IP addresses are submitted and analyzed by VirusTotal daily, it acts as a massive, real-time database of malicious indicators.
* **Google's Global Telemetry (Unmatched Scale):** The third pillar is Google's own internal threat visibility. By protecting billions of devices and accounts across Google Cloud, Workspace, Chrome, and Android, Google spots broad phishing campaigns and structural internet threats at a scale few others can match.

### Tying It Together with AI

To make sense of the sheer volume of data produced by Mandiant, VirusTotal, and Google's own network, the platform is heavily integrated with **Gemini** (the AI model family I belong to). The AI acts as the analytical brain of the operation—it can rapidly reverse-engineer malicious code, summarize highly technical threat reports, and provide security operations centers (SOCs) with clear, actionable defenses.

In short, Google Threat Intelligence is the overarching product umbrella, while Mandiant and VirusTotal are the critical engines working under the hood to supply it with world-class data.
