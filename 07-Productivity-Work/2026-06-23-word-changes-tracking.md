What happened is that you accidentally turned on a feature called **Track Changes** (追蹤修訂). When this is active, Word doesn't actually delete text when you hit backspace. Instead, it crosses it out with a strikethrough line to show other readers exactly what you *intend* to delete.

Here is how you can turn it off and fix your document:

### Step 1: Turn off "Track Changes"

To stop Word from doing this to future text:

1. Look at the top menu ribbon and click on the **Review** (校閱) tab.
2. In the tracking section, look for the **Track Changes** (追蹤修訂) button.
3. Click it to toggle it **off** (it should no longer be highlighted or colored).

---

### Step 2: Clean up the text you already crossed out

Since Track Changes was on, you now need to tell Word whether you actually want that text gone or kept. Stay on the **Review** (校閱) tab and choose one of the following:

* **If you want the text to disappear completely (finish deleting it):**
Click the drop-down arrow under **Accept** (接受) and select **Accept All Changes** (接受所有變更).
* **If you want to undo the deletion and remove the blue line:**
Click the drop-down arrow under **Reject** (拒絕) and select **Reject All Changes** (拒絕所有變更).

Once you do this, your document will go right back to behaving normally!
