That far-left vertical strip you are looking at is called the **Ribbon** in Obsidian.

Here is how the layout works for the Ribbon versus the Sidebar panels:

### The Short Answer

You **cannot drag a panel tab directly into the Ribbon**. In Obsidian, the Ribbon is strictly reserved for button shortcuts/action triggers, while the top bar of the sidebar is where the actual panels live.

### The Good News

If you look closely at the Ribbon on the far-left side of your screen in `image_7fa444.png`, **the Git icon is already there!** It is the 8th icon down (the one that looks like a source control branch, right above the AI robot icon).

Because it's already a button on your Ribbon, you can use it to toggle the panel cleanly:

1. **Hide the top tab:** If you don't want the Git tab cluttering the top bar next to your folders, you can drag it to a different sidebar or right-click it to close/hide it.
    
2. **Toggle from the Ribbon:** Whenever you click that 8th icon on the Ribbon, the Git panel will automatically slide open or close on the left side.
    

### How to move the Git button higher up the Ribbon

If you want that Git button to be at the very top of the leftest side instead of down at the 8th slot, you can easily rearrange your Ribbon:

1. **Right-click** anywhere on an empty space inside that far-left Ribbon.
    
2. Select **Configure ribbon...** from the context menu.
    
3. A menu will pop up allowing you to drag and drop the icons into whatever order you like. Just drag the Git icon to the very top!
