### Security Operations & Response

* **SOC / Security Analyst:** Acts as the primary line of defense. They continuously monitor network traffic, system logs, and security tools (like a SIEM) for suspicious activity, triage incoming alerts, and escalate confirmed threats.
* **Incident Responder:** Steps into action when a security breach or attack is confirmed. Their primary job is to investigate the scope of the incident, contain the damage, eradicate the threat from the network, and guide the recovery process.

### Engineering & Detection

* **Detection Engineer:** Focuses on creating and tuning the logic that catches malicious actors. They write and test the alerts that the SOC Analysts monitor, aiming to accurately detect threats while minimizing false positives.
* **Security Automation Engineer / Software Engineer:** Writes scripts, playbooks, and internal tools to automate repetitive security tasks (such as enriching alerts with external data or blocking malicious IP addresses). This helps the security team operate much faster and more efficiently.

### Threat Intelligence & Research

* **CTI (Cyber Threat Intelligence) Analyst:** Gathers and analyzes data about adversaries. They study who the attackers are, their motivations, and the specific tactics, techniques, and procedures (TTPs) they use to breach systems.
* **Security / Threat Researcher:** Conducts deep-dive investigations into new vulnerabilities, emerging exploits, or novel attack methods. They often discover zero-day vulnerabilities, analyze broad cyber trends, and publish security advisories.
* **Threat Hunter:** Operates under the assumption that the network is already compromised. They proactively and manually search through system logs and network data to find advanced, hidden threats that have successfully evaded automated detection systems.
* **Malware Analyst or Reverse Engineer:** Deconstructs malicious software (like ransomware, trojans, or spyware) in an isolated, safe environment. They reverse-engineer the code to understand exactly how the malware functions, what it targets, and how to build technical defenses against it.

### Leadership & Strategy

* **Manager / Executive:** Oversees the day-to-day operations of security teams (like a SOC Manager) or directs the overarching security strategy of the entire organization (like a Chief Information Security Officer). They handle budgets, compliance, hiring, and risk management.
