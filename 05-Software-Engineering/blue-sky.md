The phrase **"Blue-Sky"** is a common English idiom used in business, engineering, and creative brainstorming.

Think of it this way: when you look up at a clear, blue sky, there are **no clouds, no boundaries, and no limits**. You can see infinitely far.

Therefore, **"Blue-Sky Thinking"** means:

* **Thinking without limits:** Imagining ideas without worrying about money, current technology, time constraints, or "the way things have always been done."
* **Highly creative and innovative:** Focusing on what is *possible* in a perfect world, rather than what is easy to do right now.
* **No bad ideas:** In a blue-sky session, every wild, futuristic, or crazy idea is welcome because it might lead to a brilliant breakthrough.

In the context of your **Malcolm** project, a "Blue-Sky" prompt tells the AI: *"Don't just give me standard, boring setup steps. Give me the most advanced, futuristic ideas you can think of—like using local AI agents and edge computing—even if they are difficult to build!"*

It is the perfect approach for a project aiming to build something completely new!
