Your definition captures the framework's core purpose perfectly. The **Cybersecurity Capacity Maturity Assessment (C2MA)** goes beyond traditional IT audits by shifting the focus from technical checklists to quantified business risk.

While standard frameworks (like NIST, ISO, or CMMC) are essential, they can be rigid. C2MA is designed to measure the *actual* enterprise capacity and capability of an organization, translating complex cyber posture into data-driven business intelligence for leadership.

Here is a breakdown of how the C2MA framework operates and why organizations use it as a strategic bridge.

## How the Framework Operates

Rather than relying purely on automated network scans or static spreadsheets, C2MA takes an enterprise-wide, interview-based approach.

* **The Adaptive Risk Model (ARM):** Cyturus uses this proprietary model to evaluate the implementation status of more than **500 best practices** across the organization.
* **Interview-Driven:** Assessors speak directly with staff, HR, and business leaders—not just the IT department. This uncovers discrepancies between what a policy states and how the workforce actually operates.
* **The Cybersecurity Maturity Index (CMI):** The assessment culminates in the CMI, an algorithmically generated numerical score. This gives executive leadership a concrete, trackable metric to measure progress over time and justify resource allocation.

## The 15 Functional Domains

To ensure holistic coverage, C2MA evaluates practices across 15 distinct functional domains. This ensures that blind spots outside the traditional IT perimeter are addressed. Key domains include:

* **Workforce Management:** Evaluating how well the organization develops a culture of cybersecurity and assigns security responsibilities to individual roles.
* **Supply Chain & Vendor Risk Management:** Assessing the risks associated with third-party vendors and external dependencies.
* **Cloud & Transformational Security:** Managing the strategy and governance of data moving beyond local infrastructure into SaaS and cloud environments.
* **Threat & Vulnerability Management:** The operational ability to identify and analyze infrastructural threats.
* **Event & Incident Response:** The organizational readiness to detect, manage, and sustain operations through a live cybersecurity incident.

That single control then automatically maps its evidence and maturity status across all relevant regulatory frameworks simultaneously.
